- **Reporting and dashboards, completed (issue #34; database 2.6.223 and 2.6.224).** Everything below is additive; existing reports, saved views and schedules behave as before. Nothing is switched on by this change.
  - **PDF reports.** Every catalogued report now has **Download PDF** in its toolbar (`?export=pdf`) next to Export CSV and Print. The PDF is made on the server with the TCPDF already bundled in `plugins/TCPDF` (no browser, no extra program) from the same tables as the CSV, so the three always agree; it states the date range and the filters used. A new schedule delivery, **PDF attached to the email**, sends the PDF as a real attachment: the mail queue can now carry attachments (`email_queue_attachments`, removed as soon as the message is sent or its queue row is deleted).
  - **Quarterly schedules.** A new **Quarterly** frequency (due again 3 months after the last send) for every schedule type, including the original headline summaries.
  - **Reports hub headline row.** Open Tickets, Mean Resolution Time (30 days), SLA Compliance (30 days), Tickets This Month and User Satisfaction (30 days, when CSAT is on), then Unassigned and Opened Today. All respect the viewer's department restriction.
  - **Universal report filters.** Service Desk and SLA, Tickets, Tickets: Day by Day, Time by Technician, Technician Performance and Customer Satisfaction (and the new SLA and backlog reports) now filter by **department, location, technician, category, priority, status and asset**. The **Filters** button in the report toolbar opens the panel; the filters are saved in saved views, replayed by schedules and shown on printed and PDF output. Values are validated against fixed lists or whole numbers, and combine with (never widen) a user's department restriction.
  - **New reports.** **SLA Breach and Aging** (response and resolution breaches in a date range, compliance, breaches by priority, technician and department, how long open tickets have been overdue, the breached tickets), **Ticket Backlog** (open tickets by age, status, priority, technician and department, a weekly backlog trend, the oldest open tickets) and **Asset Lifecycle and Warranty** (warranty expired or expiring, device age, replacement candidates, by type and department). Each is in the hub, the side menu, saved views, schedules, CSV and PDF.
  - **Recipient groups and per-group views.** On Scheduled Reports, create **recipient groups** (for example Executives, Service desk) and let one schedule send a different **saved view** to each group. Addresses must be active agents or administrator-approved recipients, checked when the group is saved and again each time cron sends. A failing group is named in the schedule's status without resending the groups that succeeded.
  - **Tests.** Six new test files under `tests/i34_*`: migrations (fresh vs upgraded `SHOW CREATE TABLE`), filters and PDF, report queries on fixture data, the existing report helpers with filters on, the scheduler with recorders, and a real SMTP hand-off to a throwaway local listener proving the PDF arrives byte for byte.
  **Deploy notes:** take a database dump first, then run the database update. Two additive, idempotent steps: 2.6.223 (`email_queue_attachments`) and 2.6.224 (`report_recipient_groups`, `report_schedule_targets`). No new settings, cron entries or permissions. Verify on the live mail setup that one PDF schedule arrives with its attachment (a real mailbox is the only thing the tests could not reach).
