### Added
- Employee portal (issue #32): announcements (new Settings > Portal announcements page; all departments or one; schedule, expiry, pin, plain text) shown at the top of the employee home and in a new notification feed with an unread badge.
- Help articles (popular this month, new and updated) on the employee home; portal article reads are counted.
- My devices shows a card per device with OS, last seen, warranty and restart-needed.
- Employee API: `/api/v1/portal_auth`, `portal_home`, `portal_devices`, `portal_checklist`, `portal_announcements`, `portal_notifications` with their own hashed tokens (not usable on the staff API). Not yet verified against a live install.

### Changed
- The employee home no longer shows the stat tiles (department administrators keep the original page). Two new home sections, `announcements` and `kb`, are added to existing installs that had chosen their sections.
- Database 2.6.215-2.6.218 (new tables `portal_announcements`, `kb_article_views`, `portal_api_tokens`, `portal_feed_state`; settings column default).
