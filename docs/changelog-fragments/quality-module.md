- **Quality module: the integration with the separate quality management system (issues #52 and #55; database 2.6.261 and 2.6.262).** Everything is **off** until an administrator turns it on. RivetIT does not become a QMS: it shows each person their own items from the QMS, sends it changes, and signs people in to it.
  - **Administration > Quality** (new): the module switch (also under Settings > Modules), the QMS address (https only, checked against SSRF when saved and before every call, connection pinned to the vetted address, no redirects), client id and secret (sealed, write-only), **Test connection**, two signing secrets (shown once), the departments and the features that are enabled, timeouts and sync intervals, **Sync health** (ok / degraded / failing, with reasons), **Run sync now**, **Queue full resync**, the outbound and inbound event lists with **Replay** for dead-lettered events, and the **integration error log** (no secrets or personal data; the same correlation id the QMS logs).
  - **Department portal:** a **Quality** tab with My tasks, Corrective actions, Documents, Training, Notifications and History, plus a **Department view** for managers (overview, corrective actions, overdue tasks, training compliance, pending acknowledgments). Items are read live for the signed-in person and shown with the time they were read; when the QMS cannot be reached the page says so with a reference and shows nothing rather than stale data. Acknowledging a document or answering a corrective action asks for the password again unless the sign-in is fresh. **Kiosk:** counts, required training and document acknowledgment with the PIN; corrective actions never appear on a shared screen.
  - **QMS client with a per-user context:** OAuth 2.0 client credentials; every request for a person's items also carries a 60 second signed context (ES256, the SSO provider's key) naming that person, their department and role; the client refuses a person's request without one, and the QMS must authorize per person. Bounded retries with backoff, timeouts, a 1 MiB response cap and correlation ids.
  - **Events:** signed (HMAC-SHA256, 5 minute window), versioned, deterministic ids. RivetIT queues `lms.assignment.*`, `lms.completion.*`, `employee.updated`, `employee.deactivated` and `department.updated` (identifiers only, only for the enabled departments) with retry, dead letter, replay, a daily reconciliation window and a manual full resync. New inbound endpoint `POST /api/v1/integrations/qms/events` (scope `quality:events`, also signed, replay-protected) stores the QMS's own ids for people and departments (never matched by name) and lets the QMS ask for a resync.
  - **Single sign-on (#55):** the Quality module switch and the department list now gate the OpenID Connect provider (`module_disabled`, `department_not_enabled`); the portal "Open Quality system" button and the administrator menu entry follow. The seed row of `oidc_provider_settings` is now in `db.sql` (a fresh install could not switch the provider on).
  - **Docs and tests:** `docs/QUALITY_CONTRACT.md` is the exact contract the QMS must implement; `docs/integrations/qms-configuration.md` and `qms-troubleshooting.md` are new; OpenAPI and `docs/API.md` list the new endpoint. `tests/quality_module.php` (mock QMS in `tests/support/mock_qms.php`) and `tests/quality_ssrf.php`.

Nothing is switched on. New cron entry `cron/quality_worker.php` (every 5 minutes, written by `deploy/install.sh`; exits at once while the module is off); on an existing server add it to the cron file or re-run the installer's cron step. Not verified against the real QMS (Datum): see the go-live checklist in `docs/integrations/qms-configuration.md`.
